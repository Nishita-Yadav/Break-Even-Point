Uses and Limitations

Uses
  • Pricing decisions
  • Production planning
  • Make-or-buy choices
  • Setting sales targets
  • Judging risk of a new product
Limitations
  • Assumes constant selling price
  • Assumes clean fixed and variable split
  • Assumes a single product
  • Ignores stock and market changes

Quick Quiz

Fixed cost ₹60,000, selling price ₹50, variable cost ₹30 per unit. What is the break-even point in units?

Conclusion

  • Break-even tells us the minimum sales needed to avoid loss
  • BEP = Fixed Cost ÷ Contribution per unit
  • Margin of safety measures the cushion above BEP
  • A key tool for managers in pricing and planning
